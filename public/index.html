WikiWars is a race for two through Wikipedia. Both players start on article A and try to reach article B by clicking only the blue links inside the articles.

Making a room

The host types a name, picks the Wikipedia language (Kazakh, Russian or English), the categories and the difficulty, then sends the six-letter code to a friend.

The race

Your article is on top. Your rival's page is live underneath and scrolls as they scroll. There is no search box and no back button. Red links, images and references don't count.

Abilities

Each player has two Blurs, two Blackouts and one Rewind per round. Use them when your rival is close to the target.

Winning

The first to open the target article wins the round. If the clock runs out, nobody wins.

Difficulty

Easy (4 min) and Medium (6 min): a route of 2 or 3 clicks always exists. Hard (10 min): two famous articles from different categories, such as Brown bear and Elizabeth II. Impossible (15 min): you start on a random article from anywhere in Wikipedia.

Targets come from thousands of well-known Wikipedia articles in 18 categories, from rulers and historical events to animals, space and Kazakhstan.
